BUILD YOUR OWN KEVIN: PUT IT ON EDWARDGATCH.COM
About 5 minutes. Same way you update the rest of your site (GitHub, then Vercel goes live by itself).

WHAT'S IN THIS FOLDER
  kevin/index.html        The guide as a full page. Lives at edwardgatch.com/kevin
  kevin/og.png            The preview picture people see when you share the link (Instagram DMs, iMessage, etc.)
  assets/preview-kevin.jpg The picture on the new Kevin card on your Free resources page
  resources.html          Your current Free resources page, with the Kevin card added above the other three.
                          Nothing else on it changed.

UPLOAD
1. Go to github.com and open your website's repository (the one Vercel deploys).
2. Click "Add file" > "Upload files".
3. Open this folder (kevin-guide-upload) on your computer, select everything inside it
   (the kevin folder, the assets folder and resources.html), and drag it all onto the GitHub page.
   Leave out this instructions file (HOW-TO-UPLOAD.txt). If it goes up by accident, it does no harm.
   - The kevin folder is new.
   - assets/preview-kevin.jpg is added next to your other pictures. Nothing in assets is replaced.
   - resources.html replaces the old one. That's expected.
4. At the bottom, write a message like "Add Kevin guide" and click "Commit changes".
5. Wait about a minute. Vercel puts it live automatically.

CHECK IT (on your phone and computer)
  - edwardgatch.com/kevin opens on question 1 of 4.
  - Answer the questions; "Open my guide" works; "Full guide" in the top bar works.
  - A "Copy prompt" button copies.
  - edwardgatch.com/resources.html shows the Kevin card first, and "Open the guide" goes to the guide.
  - Send yourself the link in an Instagram DM or text. The preview should show the gold "Build your own Kevin" picture.
    (Instagram and others can take a while to refresh previews. If an old preview shows, wait and try again later.)

VISITORS
  Vercel > your project > Analytics shows visits to /kevin like your other pages.

IF SOMETHING LOOKS WRONG
  In GitHub, open the file's history and click "Revert", or upload your old resources.html again.
  The guide page is separate, so deleting the kevin folder removes it completely.
