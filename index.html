You're not as alone as you think.

A lot of people my age and younger are ambitious and really hungry. They're just not seen. They think the self-development journey is something you do alone.

It isn't. There are communities full of people who want you to win, sometimes more than you do. I felt that in Kelowna, with the friends I made there and Dan Martell's team, and I left inspired. I want you to feel that too.
