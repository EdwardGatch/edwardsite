Story

The long way to the other side.

Your environment is your greatest investment. Closed doors don't open themselves.

  1. 2022

    Leaving home

    After high school I followed my dad into architecture because I liked drawing. I moved to Vancouver as an international student to study Architectural and Building Technology at BCIT.

  2. 2023–24

    The switch

    A year in, I saw it takes 10+ years to become a credible architect. My timeline is short, so I switched to construction management and graduated with distinction. The job board only led to big firms, so I cold called and cold emailed until someone said yes. Site laborer in July 2024, Project Coordinator by September.

  3. Jan 2026

    The hardest stretch

    I started posting every day while the job turned repetitive. I nearly quit three times in six months. My mind gave up, but routine kept me picking up the camera.

  4. Jul 2026

    Changing my environment

    I joined Creator College, defined who I'm talking to, and switched to simple formats. 1.3K to 6K+ followers in about 16 weeks.

  5. Oct 2026

    Now

    I left my job on October 19 to pursue my own career. 600+ videos posted, and I'm talking to strangers every day to sharpen how I speak.
